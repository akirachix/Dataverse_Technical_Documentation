## Our Core Features

### Risk Scoring Engine
The system calculates a cumulative score to classify users into Low, Medium, or High risk level categories according to the user's symptom input. This engine’s underlying decision logic aligns directly with the Zambian Ministry of Health Antenatal care Guidelines for a Positive Pregnancy Experience and co-established WHO Pregnancy, Childbirth, Postpartum, and Newborn (PCPNC) framework.

**Technology used:** Rule-based Engine

### AI chatbot post assessment support
The AI chatbot provides pregnant mothers with instant, personalized pregnancy support through conversational interface. It answers pregnancy related questions, offers recommendations based on the mother’s pregnancy stage and risk level, and provides guidance on nutrition, self care and antenatal care.

**Technology used:** Google Gemini Flash 2.0, accessed via the Gemini API on Gemini Cloud

### Community
Under this feature, mothers can seamlessly connect with peers, chat in real-time, and share personal experiences throughout their journeys. It acts as a dedicated space for community support, enabling users to build meaningful relationships, exchange advice, and discuss shared milestones or challenges in an open, interactive forum.

Beyond standard peer-to-peer chat and experience sharing, the feature hosts a variety of interactive, flexible weekly challenges that focus on e.g. nutrition, collective milestones, and personal well-being.

**Technology used:** FastAPI with Websocket group chat rooms.
